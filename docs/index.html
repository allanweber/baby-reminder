Everything, one app

Built for the 3am questions

Feeds, appointments, diapers and weight — tracked in one calm place so you can trust your gut again.

Feed countdown & reminders
Set your feeding interval and get a live countdown to the next one — with quick +5/15/30 min nudges when life gets in the way.
Daily insights & reports
A filterable timeline of every feed, reminder and diaper change, with totals and averages by day.
Reminders for everything
Medicine, vitamins, tummy time, bath & more — daily or interval-based. Missed one? Mark it done late, no guilt.
Diaper tracking
Log color and amount in two taps — with gentle flags for shades worth mentioning to your pediatrician.
Dark mode built in
Easy on the eyes for every 3am feed — switch it on once in Settings and it remembers.
Quick log
Tap once to log vitamins, tummy time or a bath the moment it happens — no form, no schedule required.
Appointments
Doctor visits and vaccinations with a heads-up the day or hour before, plus an alarm at the time — in a list or a calendar. Reschedule or mark done in a tap.
Weight tracking
Log weigh-ins over time and see steady growth at a glance, with the change since last time — in kg or lb.
Feed tags
Tag a feed "fussy", "spit up" or "good latch" — your tags are saved and reused, and show up right in the feed list and report.
